Things to try

  • Press A: multiples · B: random, turn the sound on and listen to A⇄B. A fuses into one pitched tone; B sounds like a cluster or a bell.
  • Look at both at 10 ms: A repeats the same shape over and over, while B never quite repeats. At 1 s or 10 s, B shows a slow, uneven swell (beating) where A is a steady band.
  • Type 200, 201 into a sound and set the window to 10 s: two close frequencies beat once per second.
  • Try fundamental removed: B has no energy at the base frequency, but you still hear the same pitch, and its waveform still repeats at the same period.
  • Record a steady “ah”, press Use as base frequency, make 20 multiples, then set each amplitude until A's dots sit on the voice's peaks in the dB spectrum. Compare with Voice⇄A.
  • In Gesture, raise Stretch slowly: the harmonics drift off whole-number ratios and the sound turns metallic.
