How to

Everything you hear is made of harmonics (sine waves at 1×, 2×, 3× the pitch) and noise (hiss), then shaped by a filter. Press Play at the top to hear it.

BuildMake a sound by hand

  1. Set the Pitch.
  2. Drag across the Harmonics bars, or press a shape.
  3. Add Noise, and turn the Filter on to shape it.
  4. Use Gestures to play it with the touch pad, phone tilt or your camera.

VoiceRecord and rebuild a vowel

  1. Turn the microphone on, press Record, hold a vowel 2–3 s, press Stop.
  2. Under Compare, listen to Original, Rebuilt or Switch.
  3. Move Change pitch to hear the same vowel higher or lower.
  4. Name it and press + Save sound to keep it in My sounds.

By handCopy your vowel with the bars

  1. After recording, press Edit by hand.
  2. Small ticks on the bars show your voice's harmonics.
  3. Press Clear, then drag each bar up to its tick.
  4. Compare in Voice with Switch.

FilterVowels from a flat source

  1. After recording, press Rebuild with a filter: every harmonic at full strength, shaped by bands fitted to your voice.
  2. Or build it yourself: Flat shape, Filter On, + Add band, and move the bands.

Fricativesshh, fff, sss, jjj

  1. Record yourself saying the sound in Voice, so its curve shows on the spectrum.
  2. In Build, set Harmonics Level to 0 and raise Noise.
  3. Turn the Filter on and move bands until the noise matches your curve.
  4. For jjj (a buzzing shh), bring the harmonics back in under the noise.
  5. Back in Voice, Switch compares your recording with what you built.

MorphThe vowel square

  1. Save a few vowels to My sounds; each becomes a dot.
  2. Press and drag in the square: pitch and harmonics are blended by distance.
  3. By number blends harmonic 1 with 1, 2 with 2…; By shape blends the vowel shapes.
  4. Move dots to arrange them. Gestures can move you through the square too.
